How to Play: Laalpari

1. Goal: Control the electric Blue Dot (🔵) and survive round after round in the 3×3 Spider Arena!

2. 2-Second Timer: Every round lasts exactly 2.00 seconds. Watch the progress bar drain!

3. 7 Red vs 2 Safe Black: In every round, 7 blocks turn into dangerous Red Zones and only 2 blocks are Safe Black Zones.

4. Scanning: When the 2s timer reaches 0.00s, the arena scans your location. If you are in a Safe Black Zone, you earn +1 Level and +1 Point! If caught in a Red Zone, you lose 1 Lifeline.

5. 3 Spider Lifelines: You start with 3 lifelines (🕷️). When dropping from 2 to 1 lifeline, the Spider-Sense Prediction Round activates! Guess 1 of the 9 blocks that will be safe next round to recover +1 Lifeline!

6. College Leaderboard: Your high score binds to your unique College ID and syncs globally in real time via Supabase PostgreSQL.
